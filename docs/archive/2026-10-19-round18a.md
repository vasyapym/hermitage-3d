## Verdicts
Accepted with two orchestrator corrections.

Centripetal Catmull-Rom (α = ½) through the stops with END PHANTOMS ONLY. The specialist's argued deviation from §6.1's «two helper points per flight» was adopted and §6.1 amended: centripetal is cusp-free from the stop data alone, and the frozen contracts carry no numbers to justify extras.
Stops snap bit-identical (STOP_EPS).
The committed pose law is exact at stops, with bounding yaw/pitch angle-blended between (shortest arc). Limits blend linearly and re-clamp on settle.
Glide: step 1400 ms / scroll 320 ms, with in-flight retarget (step) / extend (scroll). Reduced-motion cuts.
Wheel/drag/arrow INTENTS. Boundary intents come only from arrow steps (scroll clamps silently, since inertia must not fire airlocks).
Seed precedence ?stop= > ?cam= > entry. The ?cam= parser mirrors ?stop=.
CORRECTION 1 (golden-pose catch): the square's committed framing is the authored S0 lookAt, NOT the room pose law, so noon/white-night would have broken. Added an optional per-stop gaze override to samplePose + SQUARE_S0_GAZE (S1 gazes into the arch, host-resolved to the gallery entry stop; §6.1 amended).
CORRECTION 2 (restore): the MASKED protocol worked. S0/S1 ids were restored to the §6.1 naming ('S0'/'S1', not the specialist's words) with §6.1 values verbatim.
