## Verdicts
Accepted near-wholesale on the first draw, two orchestrator corrections.

src/camera/look.ts: the look vocabulary moves there (LookOffset/LookLimits/NO_LOOK + clampLook verbatim §6.1) with two declared tuning laws:

Sensitivity deg/px = DRAG_SPAN_DEG / viewportHeightPx (viewport-FRACTION-invariant across screens, isotropic). CORRECTION: DRAG_SPAN_DEG 40 → 35, calibrated to the committed §6.1 yaw limit per the specialist's own challenge.
Frame-rate-independent exponential damping (LOOK_TAU_MS 100, self-similar: one 100 ms step ≡ ten 10 ms steps, pinned by test).
easeLook hands back the SAME object when settled (the bit-identity lever); LOOK_SNAP_DEG 1e-3 lands the ease exactly.


rail.ts: RailState gains lookTarget. Drag accumulates the clamped TARGET; the rendered look eases in advance(), folded into the existing per-frame call with the at-rest reference fast path. Settle re-clamps both as limits blend per frame.

main.ts: the drag handler switched to tracked clientX/clientY deltas (movementX is unreliable on touch; iOS often 0). The grab-the-world sign law is unchanged. pointerId-matched anchor reset on up/cancel; canvas.clientHeight passed through.
style.css: touch-action: none on #scene (touch pointermove without page scroll).
