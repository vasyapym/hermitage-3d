one lamp field, three mouths

The gallery's three states become one lamp field differing only where daylight/glow lands.
DAY = night + day rigs composed LINEARLY at scale 1. By linearity of light transport this is the joint bake fixtures-ON + sun + day world.
The gallery carries NO pane geometry (PANE_MATERIAL_NAMES is jordan's), so the composite is the joint bake up to OIDN's nonlinearity only — stronger than the reply assumed.


the shared-white-point law

All three states encode at white point 0.2. The manifest's intensity record was edited first, then re-encoded.
The lamp field is byte-identical across the three atlases, so technique A's seam moves only the daylight/glow texels.


the ceiling law

max eff = 0.6 = the accepted dusk (gains day 3 / dusk 3 / night 2.75). No state exposes a coarser 8-bit step than the accepted dusk.
Night ≤ day holds PER TEXEL, not just on mean.


The band rule spent its step per its own law

The rule is exhausted at its ceiling g(night) < g(day).

The sconces gate made precise (the reply's design)
