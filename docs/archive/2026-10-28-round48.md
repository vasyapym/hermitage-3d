## Verdicts
THE DIRECTION:

A native <select> sits in the clock row (the last item of #timeline, the timeline's register). It is a DATE control at season granularity, NOT a snow switch. It reads «Сейчас» live and the season's name when fixed.
Choosing a season moves the scene date to that season's anchor day. SEASON_ANCHORS: winter Jan 15 on the plateau / spring May 15 / summer Jun 21 / autumn Oct 15. snowWeight is exactly 1 at the winter anchor and exactly 0 at the other three (the invariant unit-tested).
It uses the same local civil year, with the local time of day preserved.
It is committed through the scrub's transaction: fixed time, one replaceState writing canonical at=, det + unrelated keys preserved, no preview, no new hash key, NOTHING persisted (the URL already carries the result).
«Сейчас» = the scrub's return-to-now law verbatim.
Alternatives were compared against a fixed rubric:

The snow override was REJECTED outright (a third wSnow writer + a season axis on the goldens).
The two-state toggle survives as the fallback (it degenerates by deleting two anchor rows).
The year-wide scrub +  was rejected (arbitrary dates already exist via at=).


State law: seasonOf by the meteorological seasons in LOCAL +03 (pure, the utcCivil pattern). The select's value is DERIVED from the committed TimeState, re-derived per commit and hashchange. There is no season state anywhere; delete the control and the system is unchanged.
