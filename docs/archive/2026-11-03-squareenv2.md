### DEC-038 — The Column's enclosure, Part A: the sourced 1834 bronze fence lands as one merged bronze mesh — twelve upright trophy guns, staffs at a derived 0.45 m pitch, closed centred gates; the lanterns stay the named successor

Form: the kit's abstraction after Montferrand.

One merged non-indexed Lambert mesh.
Bronze BRONZE_PATINA_LINEAR borrowed by reference (no new colour, no new shader slot, no program key).
ColumnFenceBronze / AlexanderColumnFence, casting and receiving shadow.
Rides the massing fade, mirrored beside setColumnOpacity (the Column's own state posture).


SOURCED (wiki-ru «Александровская колонна» — «Характеристики», «Ограда и окружение памятника», «Ремонт ограды»):

Envelope 16.5 × 16.5 × 1.5.
136 double-headed eagles.
12 trophy guns (4 at the corners + 2 per gate on each of the 4 sides); between them alternating spears and banner-staffs.
Installed 1834, complete 1836–1837.
Dismantled and melted in the 1930s; the 2004 replica is a «решётка», so the modelled fence is the original, not the replica.


DECLARED:

Guns as upright cylinders at the fence's full height, r 0.15 m / 8 segments, the gate guns at ±1.5 m from each side's midpoint.
Staffs r 0.025 m / 4 segments.
Two square ties per side, 0.06 m, at y 0.15 / 1.35 (the staffs clear 0.12 m).
Each gate centred, two closed leaves of three staff cells (6 cells).
Railing line 8.1 m.
The 136 eagles read as one crowned staff each.


DERIVED: the staff pitch 0.45 m.
NOT IN THE SOURCE, SO NOT MODELLED: a base or kerb, the finish, the gun profile, the eagle and pike forms, the spears' own count, the locks, the guard booth.
Rail clearances, corrected:

The rail line's perpendicular clearance is 4.70635 m to the fence's NW corner and 8.34997 m to the SW corner.
The rail's closest point to the NW corner is ≈(−12.84016, 180.71072), ≈3.80 m before Sh.
These sharpen DEC-037's «≈2.9 m» read, which subtracted the half-diagonal 11.67 from the axis clearance 14.57449 as a bound; the axis-aligned square's actual corners sit farther off the rail.
All three stops are bit-identical (S0, Sh, S1 untouched).


Capture read (det, S0): at noon the fence grounds the monument — railing and ties legible, rising past the pedestal at the corners. At dec-night it reads as a dark silhouette over the lit facade band. Night: unlit (the lanterns are the successor).
Lantern successor note, the hunt's sharpest facts:

The count «four» appears in the sources only in the 1977 recreations — «воссозданы в первоначальных формах четыре фонаря по её углам» — of the SQUARE's corners, not the Column's.
The 1876 sentence names the architect К. К. Рахау with candelabra at the Column.
The 1834 project already suggested «канделябры с медными фонарями и газовым освещением».
Lantern height and form are absent everywhere, so no lantern count, height or form is sourced yet.


Rejected: none this round — the Part A reply was adopted verbatim and no alternative form was tabled.
