### DEC-013 — Rooms: a lazy-module registry, the square stays resident

Verdict: rooms/ holds one folder per room (contract + module + generated manifest), registered in one list.
The square is not a room: no manifest and no disposal scope. Its facade kit, massing and shadow pass flip to visible=false indoors under registry control.
Reason: the default route must paint with zero fetches, and a resident cannot honestly carry a disposal scope (docs/design-staircase.md §1.1).
Rejected alternatives:

Square as room 0.
Modelling the real ~200 m courtyard walk (tag n/r). Declared theatre-set compression carries the visitor instead.
