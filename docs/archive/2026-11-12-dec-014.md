### DEC-014 — Airlock is a neighbor-edge seam, not a room

Verdict: airlocks own portals, transit kinds (doorway/cut; reduced-motion forces cut) and the only legal dispose/swap hooks.

Dispose happens on exit.
The environment/exposure/post swap happens in the hold.
Airlocks have no assets, no route key, and are never a camera destination (docs/design-staircase.md §2.4).


Rejected alternatives:

Airlock-as-room: it burns lifetime rooms on corridors nobody stops in.
Dispose possible mid-transit: it breaks the ledger assertion.


By-construction claims: none recorded.
