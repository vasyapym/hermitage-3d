### DEC-017 — Every transit opens on a dark title card — the room title above the entry caption, a minimum dwell, never a spinner

Rewritten 2026-10-22 in the walkthrough UX pass, R-UX1: the cold-load card became the universal threshold rule.
The transit is depart → black title card → arrive.

The light drains through renderer.toneMappingExposure (no overlay).
The swap hides inside the black.
The arrival glide is reached, never cut to (design-walkthrough-ux.md §4).
Reduced motion keeps two cuts with the card's dwell.


The card's minimum dwell is the repurposed hold constant, exposed as data-hold-ms.
A hold >2 s (tag n/r) on the device is a budget failure to fix, not to hide. It is attacked by the arrival-settle prefetch (design-walkthrough-ux.md §4.6).
Law: docs/design-staircase.md §1.2, §8.4.

Progress bars.
Frozen fades: a camera that stops while the light drains reads as loading.
Landing on the square and auto-gliding away from the requested URL.
