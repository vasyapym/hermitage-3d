## Verdicts
### DEC-019 — Night exterior: lit windows ARE the night facade; hemisphere ground warm-up is the only secondary

Primary: the square's night view of the palace is the interior nightChandeliers state seen from outside.

There is one emissive pane per window (new instanced quads), because facade recesses are opaque dark panels.
Panes are hashed (no Math.random) into three stock MeshStandardMaterial levels: dark / dim warm / bright warm, with one chandelier-matched hue.
emissiveIntensity = kLevel × wNight each frame. wNight is the §8 weight imported, not re-derived: one clock curve so inside and outside agree.
Interiors' own windows read bright (jordan's emissive pane row, DEC-018).


Secondary: hemi.groundColor lerps toward a warm up-light tint on the same weight.

This gives a faint inverted-shading warmth on cornice undersides while the sky-facing square stays cold.


Seasonal by construction:

June-solstice midnight bottoms at −6.6° (tag n/r), inside the dusk plateau, weight 0. White-night windows stay dark under a still-luminous sky.
December's deep night gets the full lattice.


Ships as Phase 2b: a small standalone step after Phase 2, before Phase 3. The sun is below −12° (tag n/r) before 19:00 November–January, so every evening visitor currently meets a black palace.
Budget (tags n/r): +≤ 3 draws, +≤ ~4 k tris (~1 900 panes worst case per DEC-004), 0 custom slots, +0 lights.
Must not touch: the three-state contract, facade kit materials (slot reserved for snow), the day/dusk sky palette, light counts, UI.
Risks carried:

Stone may still read too dark (the 5+ menu item fixes that).
The ~35/40/25 level split is a tuning target.
Interiors share hemi: layer-mask or drop the secondary if interior night goldens shift.


Rejected alternatives:

Real floodlights / RectAreaLight bar: a day-time fragment tax on Iris 640 plus a shader change. The right version is a material height gradient sharing a slot with Phase 4 snow, deferred to the 5+ "Floodlit facade" item.
Patched material with continuous GLSL hash: spends a scarce slot for marginal gain.
Lampposts with halos: modelling plus a transparent pass for a small accent (5+ menu).
Moon light: a lunar ephemeris or a lie the clock exposes.
Bloom: T2-only by DEC-015.
A fourth baked state or flicker: a §8 contract violation and golden noise.


By-construction claim: the seasonal behaviour above.
