## Verdicts
### DEC-031 — The boundary has two roads; the URL follows the room

(a) A sustained deliberate wheel past a rail end fires that end's airlock. Pure law: src/camera/wheel-edge.ts, with three declared thresholds (the accumulated budget, the per-event speed gate, the sustain window), so a trackpad's momentum burst cannot fire it. Threshold values: n/r at pin (tag: declared).
(b) A keyed chrome prompt (chrome.edgePrompt, a real button on the free bottom-center axis) shows while settled at an enterable rail end. It travels through the same boundary chain the arrows use.
(c) The transit's completion rewrites the hash to the entered room's route (writeRoomUrl, replaceState, the stale ?stop=/?cam= dropped). The URL-is-the-state law now holds for human-entered transits too.
Rejected: wheel-through without the momentum gates (the r18 law's own reason); a persistent minimap/sidebar (UI nearly absent); history pushState per transit (wheel walks would spam the browser history).
R-UX2 amendment (2026-10-23, the specialist's ruling; design-walkthrough-ux.md §2, D1):

The prompt becomes the DOOR LABEL: verb + « · » + the room's NAME (the head of the contract's sentence title before the em dash: «Enter · Rastrelli Gallery», «Back out · Palace Square»). New keys chrome.doorEnter/chrome.doorBack replace chrome.edgePrompt.
Appearance law (the push answer): settled ∧ at rest ∧ at an enterable end ∧ (the far end reached ∨ a push into the clamp in the door's direction). The push is a pure per-frame memory over the glide-tolerant clamped-target read.
The sustained pass gains its declared END: a quiet gap EDGE_QUIET_MS = 350 between events resets the pass (lastMs in the state; per-frame wheelEdgeSettle). A paused press no longer inherits its budget, and the lean has a definite moment to spring back from. The three ruled thresholds are untouched.
Feedback: the wheel pass LEANS the camera, a sprung first-order dolly (src/camera/door-lean.ts, LEAN_MAX_M = 0.35 ∝ budget/EDGE_BUDGET_PX, rise τ 90 ms / release τ 260 ms, position and target together). It folds into the depart beat's locked door pose, so the lean continues seamlessly into the departure glide.
The lean is absent under reduced motion (the enter still fires). The hidden label is unfocusable (visibility: hidden with the fade).
