## Verdicts
### DEC-034 — The manual season chooser: the mode surface opens — a native select in the clock row that chooses the DATE (the season remains a law of the clock)

seasonOf(epochMs) reads the METEOROLOGICAL season of the LOCAL (+03) civil date: winter Dec–Feb, spring Mar–May, summer Jun–Aug, autumn Sep–Nov. This is deliberately not the snow law's seams: Mar 5 reads spring with full snow, and the date line beside the select is the truth.
Anchors (SEASON_ANCHORS; the owner's taste parameters): winter Jan 15 (deep on the snow plateau), spring May 15, summer Jun 21, autumn Oct 15.

Invariant (unit-tested): snowWeight is exactly 1 at every hour of the winter anchor and exactly 0 at every hour of the other three.


Commit: choosing a season commits seasonInstant(...), the anchor day in the scene instant's LOCAL civil year at its LOCAL time of day. It goes through the scrub's transaction: fixed time, one history.replaceState writing canonical at=, det and unrelated keys preserved, no history entry, no preview.
«Сейчас» is the scrub's return-to-now law verbatim.
The select's value is DERIVED from the committed TimeState (never stored; re-derived per commit and per hashchange; never during scrub preview).
An out-of-bounds anchor is refused (state unchanged, re-sync, one diagnostic). This is validation, not a clamp.
