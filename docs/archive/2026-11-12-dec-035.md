## Verdicts
### DEC-035 — The ground-snow look (R12): the whole 600×600 plane carries the wSnow cover, the SQUARE is distinguished by a trodden lane along the route; a SECOND material slot on the ground Lambert; the hemisphere bounce; the SNOW_FACING record restores

Extent (design-ground-snow §1, the ratification's named first resolution): cover everywhere. Rejected alternative: a white-only-square island, which would be a fidelity nobody asked for and a seam the frame cannot hide.
The lane is the trample signature: lane(x) = 1 − smoothstep(coreHalfM 10, edgeHalfM 22, |x|). It depends on world X only and is infinite along Z, so it runs under both stops, through the gate, into the courtyard.
trampleField = BASE 0.3 + (1−BASE)·lane.
snowRgb = mix(FRESH, TRAMPLED 0.38/0.39/0.41, trample). FRESH is re-exported from the season law by reference.
The ground term is ONE onBeforeCompile with customProgramCacheKey 'Ground/snow-slot@1' on the separate groundMaterial.

It is albedo only. Lambert has no roughness/metalness carrier, so the sketch's roughness was dropped honestly.
Anchors are common/worldpos_vertex/color_fragment. Verified in the three 0.186 Lambert fragment: color_fragment sits before the lighting includes, so the surface-before-light order holds.
It uses a private GLSL-bicubic smoothstep twin, NOT the repo's quintic smootherstep.
Every GLSL literal is emitted from the pure constants. The SNOW_FACING lesson is enforced by a GLSL/TS agreement test.


Hemisphere bounce (§3): snowBounceGround(h, wSnow) = lerp(0.25·h, h⊙GROUND_BOUNCE_LINEAR [0.50,0.52,0.56], wSnow), composed BEFORE the existing wNight warm.

It is endpoint-exact at both ends: dec-night's hemi is bit-unchanged, and June/Oct are bit-exact at wSnow = 0.
The r17c light seam hides sun/hemi indoors, so no interior material can receive it.
Identity at wSnow = 0 is exact: no early-out, no define. The §4.3 fallback is recorded, not implemented.


Artistic-license row 21 is amended to «the square carries trampled snow with a trodden lane along the viewer's route (the real square is machine-cleared)».
