## Verdicts
### DEC-037 — The Palace Square commits to the post-1834 square: the Alexander Column stands on the gate axis; the visitor's rail runs west of the axis and converges on it at S1

Direction: the monument the square itself is named for reorganizes the foreground, as a 47.5 m vertical between eye and wall at all three stop frames.
Form: the kit's abstraction, a lathe silhouette with no fluting (the real shaft is unfluted). It is one merged Lambert mesh with vertex colours riding the massing fade (the throat's split + setOpacity posture), with no new shader slot and no program key.
SOURCED (wiki-ru «Александровская колонна», the implementation hunt):

total 47.5
shaft 25.6, Ø 3.66 → 3.15 (the article's history section reads 3.19, recorded as the source's own variance)
plinth 3.9 on 6.3 × 6.3
pedestal 4.25 with reliefs 5.24 × 3.1
angel 4.26
cross 6.4
fence 16.5 × 16.5 × 1.5 (NOT MODELLED this round)


DERIVED: the finial band 13.75 (the sum pinned to 47.5 in the law).
Position: on the gate axis, «в центре Дворцовой площади» (the Rastrelli north–south axis fixed by Rossi; wiki-ru «Дворцовая площадь», ≈5.4 ha).
DECLARED: the 96.5 m plan distance. The clause the direction opened stands as declared (DEC-026 posture: the form has its evidence, only a plan coordinate was provisional).
Rail:

S0 [−24,1.7,230], Sh [−12,1.7,177], S1 [0,1.7,124], bit-identical with all three subjects untouched. The three stops are XZ-collinear.
The straight line's perpendicular clearance to the Column axis is 14.57449 m (≥ COLUMN_RAIL_CLEARANCE_M 12). The sourced fence half-diagonal 11.67 m keeps ≈2.9 m.
West, not east (one mirrored choice): at both noon goldens the visitor on the west sees the Column's sunlit face.


Ground snow: the trodden lane is re-centred on the rail line (GROUND_LANE_LINE near [0,124] → far [−24,230], clamped to that span; the courtyard extends the axis centre). Otherwise dec-noon shows the visitor in fresh snow off their own lane. Identity at uSnow 0 is bit-exact.
Shadows:

The committed ±130 m shadow box is re-centred on z 95 (SUN_SHADOW_FIT, halfway between the palace centre and the Column).
The light AND its target sit on a rig at that point. sun.position stays the rig-local direction × SUN_DISTANCE, so the direction is unchanged while the box translates.
The fit holds the Column's base and its finial-shadow ground point inside the footprint at the six square-covering golden instants. This was verified direction-wise at the three sun-below-horizon rows, where the base is covered too.


Two tuning colours (not sourced): GRANITE_RAPAKIVI [0.30, 0.15, 0.12] linear; BRONZE_PATINA [0.09, 0.11, 0.09].
Night: unlit (the period truth: pre-1876 lanterns).
State table: visible in every square state, fades with the massing, hidden indoors by the PalaceMassing flip.
Rejected:

displacing the monument off its axis to clear the rail;
leaving the rail on the axis with the Column behind S0;
filling the square with pavement and invented lamp standards (every lamp form would be invented, per DEC-026's posture; the real lamps are the Column's 1876 lanterns, the named next square item);
pulling S0 back toward z ≈ 275 to hold the whole Column in frame (it undoes the pinned «the facade and the hour» golden).
