# Decisions

Each decision: what, why in one line, and the credible rejected alternative. New decisions append as DEC-00x; a change of mind opens a new entry, old ones stay for the record.

- **DEC-001 · Rendering: vanilla three.js, hand-written engine layer** — one scene, a hand-tuned loop, custom materials, tiny UI; owning the small engine layer is a stronger senior signal than wiring a framework. *Rejected: React Three Fiber + drei + zustand — great for editor-like UI; a reconciler in the hot path and 2× version coupling buy nothing here.*
- **DEC-002 · WebGL2 baseline; WebGPU as a later opt-in tier** — reaches every reviewer's browser; the drama is baked light + few effects, not compute. `three/webgpu` is a moving target. Evaluated again by ADR in Phase 4, by measurement. *Rejected: WebGPU-first with WebGL fallback — months on the bleeding edge.*
- **DEC-003 · No UI framework** — six DOM elements; a typed store + explicit FSM is more legible. *Rejected: Preact overlay — a second component model for a nav and a time line.*
- **DEC-004 · Exterior: facade grammar + kit of parts** — Rastrelli's elevations are a grammar: typed bay sequences, risalits, order heights, cornice profiles → a pure solver places ~25 instanced kit meshes; ~1 900 windows become a number, not a workload. The generator itself is a portfolio artefact. *Rejected: hand-model the facades — months before anything ships; the download budget dies; no engineering story. Scanned/purchased assets rejected too: licensing murk, style incoherence, payloads.*
- **DEC-005 · Interiors: theatre sets bounded by the camera envelope** — the union of the room's rail-frusta (plus margin) is the only modelled area; rooms get interior kit + ≤ 3 hero meshes. *Rejected: fully modelled rooms — the 1 500-room palace swallows the project.*
- **DEC-006 · Assets as code** — every committed binary in `public/assets/` regenerates from a committed bpy/gltf-transform script; Blender runs locally headless, never in CI. Fits an agentic workflow and makes art reproducible, reviewable and small. *Rejected: committing .blend files with hand edits — unreproducible, unverifiable.*
- **DEC-007 · Signature feature: Petersburg Time** — the palace lives in the real sky at 59.9°N (white nights in June, a six-hour December day); interiors shift baked light states by the same clock. Culturally specific, lands in Phase 0, core is pure tested code. *Rejected: The Cut (dollhouse section) as primary — needs ≥ 2 interiors; adopted instead as a standing constraint (true coordinates) and the first v2 feature. Rastrelli's Line (ink-drawing transition) parked — risks reading as a shader demo.*
- **DEC-008 · Camera: the museum visitor** — rail + composed stops + ±35° free look. *Rejected: free roam — collision systems, full coverage, uncomposed frames.*
- **DEC-009 · Sky: hand-written analytic dome material** (2026-10-15, round open date; assignment date n/r · assigned at round-2 integration, act n/r at pin · round 2 · brief n/r, design §7) — verdicts: the dome is built around a controlled twilight continuation; Preetham (three.js Sky) is a daylight model whose below-horizon extrapolation fails the defining white-night band; rejected: three.js Sky as-is (→ archive) — contracts: white-night band −8.6°…−4.6° (tag n/r at pin); consumes 1 of the ≤ 3 custom-material slots (cap; tag n/r at pin), 2 remain (derived) — law: docs/design-sky-time.md §7 · why: docs/archive/2026-10-15-sky-time-design.md#dec-009
- **DEC-010 · No PMREM in Phase 0** (2026-10-15, round open date; assignment date n/r · assigned at round-2 integration, act n/r at pin · round 2 · brief n/r, design §7) — verdicts: exterior massing is diffuse Lambert under a solar directional light + a palette-derived hemispheric light; `scene.environment` stays null; the bake jank failure mode is removed by removing the bake, not rate-limited; rejected: PMREM regeneration on clock movement (→ archive); revisited by ADR in Phase 2 with interiors — contracts: no numeric contract; scope Phase 0 — law: docs/design-sky-time.md §7 · why: docs/archive/2026-10-15-sky-time-design.md#dec-010
- **DEC-011 · Quality tiers on §11 anchors, boot probe at the heaviest surfaces** (2026-10-17 · assigned at round-9 integration (ADR-0011 recorded) · round 9 · brief round-9 §n/r) — verdicts: T1 reproduces the §11 designated-device gate verbatim; T0 bounds it down, T2 relaxes it up (post reserved); auto tier from a lower-median rAF probe armed at T2 surfaces, decided pre-arrival so the massing compiles once; `?q=` override persists; det boots skip the probe; one heuristic only, `hardwareConcurrency` excluded (misgrades the reference device) — contracts: probe ~0.85 s (declared) · `deviceMemory < 4 GiB` → cap T0 (declared) · 2-core reference device (declared) · t0 cap 786 432 px (declared) — law: docs/adr/0011.md | here · why: docs/archive/2026-10-17-quality-tiers.md#dec-011 · *Rejected: probe at T1 surfaces — negligible differentiation; runtime watchdog downgrade — parked until golden frames exist.*
- **DEC-012 · Playwright e2e: CI smoke on software GL, goldens local-only** — the budget table's "HUD + Playwright assertion" rows run on every push (det T1/T0 boots read the HUD `data-*` contract; clean console; generous hang bounds), while pixel goldens are rendered only on the reference Mac's real GPU with committed platform-suffixed baselines — the charter's documented fallback adopted deliberately: software-GL output is deterministic per machine but baselines are not portable, and the headless compositor throttles cadence (~200 ms median vs 16.6 headed on the same build/GPU), so CI can never vouch frame cost. Round 11 also proved a locator screenshot never passes element stability on a rAF-driven WebGL canvas; goldens capture page-level with a clip ([docs/adr/0012.md](adr/0012.md)). *Rejected: CI goldens with generated software-GL baselines — double the baseline surface, still not the real GPU; runtime watchdog built this round — golden frames had to exist first.*

## Phase 2 (design-staircase.md, owner dispositions by default 2026-10-17)

- **DEC-013 · Rooms: a lazy-module registry, the square stays resident** — `rooms/` holds one folder per room (contract + module + generated manifest) registered in one list; the square is **not** a room: no manifest, no disposal scope, its facade kit/massing/shadow pass flip to `visible=false` indoors under registry control — because the default route must paint with zero fetches and a resident cannot honestly carry a disposal scope ([docs/design-staircase.md](design-staircase.md) §1.1). *Rejected: square as room 0; modelling the real ~200 m courtyard walk (declared theatre-set compression carries the visitor instead).*
- **DEC-014 · Airlock is a neighbor-edge seam, not a room** — airlocks own portals, transit kinds (`doorway`/`cut`; reduced-motion forces `cut`) and the *only* legal dispose/swap hooks (dispose on exit, environment/exposure/post swap in the hold); no assets, no route key, never a camera destination ([docs/design-staircase.md](design-staircase.md) §2.4). *Rejected: airlock-as-room — burns lifetime rooms on corridors nobody stops in; dispose possible mid-transit (breaks the ledger assertion).*
- **DEC-015 · Post stack per tier: T0/T1 none, T2 selective emissive bloom only** — bloom thresholds on the chandelier/window emissives (~13 fullscreen draws; interiors plan ≤220 scene draws so T2 holds ≤250), no SSAO on the reference GPU, no history-dependent effects because det must freeze goldens; T1 bloom only on r17 measurement ([docs/design-staircase.md](design-staircase.md) §7). *Rejected: T1 bloom by default — 16.7 ms @1080p on Iris 640 has no headroom on taste alone; SSAO; TAA/motion blur.*
- **DEC-016 · Asset pipeline pins: Blender 4.2 LTS · gltf-transform + Meshopt · KTX-Software toktx ETC1S `--threads 1` · three 0.186.x loaders; CI hashes and never re-encodes** — one committed binary regenerates byte-identically from one committed script; bake-once-and-cache with per-room bake logs; Blender never runs in CI; ETC1S for lightmaps + albedo, UASTC only where banding shows ([docs/design-staircase.md](design-staircase.md) §3). *Rejected: Draco (decoder weight for no texture story), encoding/Blender in CI (nondeterminism + runtime cost), UASTC-everything (payload).*
- **DEC-017 · Every transit opens on a dark title card — the room title above the entry caption, a minimum dwell, never a spinner** (rewritten 2026-10-22 in the walkthrough UX pass, R-UX1: the cold-load card became the universal threshold rule). The transit is depart → black title card → arrive: the light drains through `renderer.toneMappingExposure` (no overlay), the swap hides inside the black, the arrival glide is reached — never cut to (design-walkthrough-ux.md §4); reduced motion keeps two cuts with the card's dwell. The card's minimum dwell is the repurposed hold constant, exposed as `data-hold-ms`; a hold >2 s on the device is a budget failure to fix, not to hide — attacked by the arrival-settle prefetch (design-walkthrough-ux.md §4.6). ([docs/design-staircase.md](design-staircase.md) §1.2, §8.4). *Rejected: progress bars; frozen fades (a camera that stops while the light drains reads as loading); landing on the square and auto-gliding away from the requested URL.*
- **DEC-018 · Jordan windows carry the baked sky (flippable)** — opaque baked panes per state instead of see-through glass: the dome's brightness follows E(h) while interiors follow the bakes, and the card-model shell has nothing honest to see through; saves draws ([docs/design-staircase.md](design-staircase.md) §2.3). *Rejected: see-through stencils with the dome rendered indoors — real sun movement in the panes is the owner's flip and costs a draw plus an exposure reconciliation.*
- **Deferred (Q5): lightmap-blend technique** — recorded as a candidate ([docs/design-staircase.md](design-staircase.md) §4.4) and deliberately unwritten until the r17 measurement runs: presumptive default technique A (one custom lightmap-blend shader, spending custom-material slot 2 of 2) vs B-RT vs B-discrete, with the falsifiable flip thresholds in the doc. Interiors probe: **ADR-0013** ([docs/adr/0013.md](adr/0013.md)).

## Night exterior (relay round 18, chat-model decision adopted by the owner 2026-10-18)

- **ADR-0015 · MSAA on at every tier; the T1 dpr-2 raise measured and left open** — the far-field aliasing (the owner's «the more far the more blurry») was the parked no-MSAA cost; measured free on the reference device (all gate scenes at the vsync floor, SwiftShader smoke green with margins), the golden baselines re-rendered once. The T1 dpr-2 raise measured viable-but-edge (jordan 16.8–16.9 idle; an earlier 33.3 reading was machine load) — the owner disposes; `?q=2` stands as the measured sharp mode meanwhile ([docs/adr/0015.md](adr/0015.md)). *Rejected: FXAA/SMAA post (DEC-015), non-integer DPR, per-scene resolution laws.*
- **DEC-019 · Night exterior: lit windows ARE the night facade; hemisphere ground warm-up is the only secondary** — the square's night view of the palace is the interior `nightChandeliers` state seen from outside: one emissive pane per window (new instanced quads — facade recesses are opaque dark panels), hashed (no `Math.random`) into three stock `MeshStandardMaterial` levels — dark / dim warm / bright warm, one chandelier-matched hue — with `emissiveIntensity = kLevel × wNight` each frame, `wNight` the §8 weight **imported, not re-derived** (one clock curve so inside and outside agree); interiors' own windows read bright (jordan's emissive pane row, DEC-018). Secondary: `hemi.groundColor` lerps toward a warm up-light tint on the same weight — a faint inverted-shading warmth on cornice undersides while the sky-facing square stays cold. Seasonal by construction: June-solstice midnight bottoms at −6.6° — inside the dusk plateau, weight 0 — so white-night windows stay dark under a still-luminous sky; December's deep night gets the full lattice. Ships as **Phase 2b** (small standalone step after Phase 2, before Phase 3): sun is below −12° before 19:00 November–January, so every evening visitor currently meets a black palace. Budget: +≤ 3 draws, +≤ ~4 k tris (~1 900 panes worst case per DEC-004), 0 custom slots, +0 lights. Must not touch: the three-state contract, facade kit materials (slot reserved for snow), the day/dusk sky palette, light counts, UI. Risks carried: stone may still read too dark (the 5+ menu item fixes that); the ~35/40/25 level split is a tuning target; interiors share `hemi` — layer-mask or drop the secondary if interior night goldens shift. *Rejected: real floodlights / RectAreaLight bar — day-time fragment tax on Iris 640 plus a shader change; right version is a material height gradient sharing a slot with Phase 4 snow, deferred to the 5+ "Floodlit facade" item; patched material with continuous GLSL hash — spends a scarce slot for marginal gain; lampposts with halos — modelling plus a transparent pass for a small accent (5+ menu); moon light — a lunar ephemeris or a lie the clock exposes; bloom — T2-only by DEC-015; a fourth baked state or flicker — §8 contract violation and golden noise.*

## The Russian voice (relay round 23, chat-model decisions 2026-10-20)

- **DEC-020 · Locale strategy: RU default and canonical; `?lang=` joins the hash-query grammar (URL wins → the persisted override per the `?q=` lifecycle → RU, invalid ignored); one toggle showing the target language in its own script; nothing seeds from `navigator.language`; the HUD stays EN (a developer tool); data keys stay raw tokens with per-language display maps (spec hashes stable); captions and data prose become `{ru, en}` records validated at import — the fail-loud law; the RU strings ship as a lazy chunk counted by the existing ≤ 350 000 gzip total and measured separately by the budget job, never a loosening** ([docs/design-i18n-notes.md](design-i18n-notes.md) §1–§4). *Rejected: EN default (the subject and the sources are Russian; foreign links carry `?lang=en`); `navigator.language` seeding (hidden state, doubles the test matrix); two HTML trees per language; an ICU plural engine (noun-first RU templates are inflection-safe).*

- **DEC-031 · The boundary has two roads; the URL follows the room** — the owner's reachability report (2026-10-21: a trackpad visitor stalled at every door; the copied URL never named the walked room): (a) a sustained deliberate wheel past a rail end fires that end's airlock — pure law `src/camera/wheel-edge.ts` with three declared thresholds (the accumulated budget, the per-event speed gate, the sustain window) so a trackpad's momentum burst cannot fire it; (b) a keyed chrome prompt (`chrome.edgePrompt`, a real button on the free bottom-center axis) shows while settled at an enterable rail end and travels through the same boundary chain the arrows use; (c) the transit's completion rewrites the hash to the entered room's route (`writeRoomUrl`, replaceState, the stale ?stop=/?cam= dropped) — the URL-is-the-state law now holds for human-entered transits too. *Rejected: wheel-through without the momentum gates (the r18 law's own reason); a persistent minimap/sidebar (UI nearly absent); history pushState per transit (wheel walks would spam the browser history).*
  **R-UX2 amendment (2026-10-23, the specialist's ruling; design-walkthrough-ux.md §2, D1):** the prompt becomes the DOOR LABEL — verb + « · » + the room's NAME (the head of the contract's sentence `title` before the em dash: «Enter · Rastrelli Gallery», «Back out · Palace Square»; new keys `chrome.doorEnter`/`chrome.doorBack` replace `chrome.edgePrompt`), and the appearance law is the push answer: settled ∧ at rest ∧ at an enterable end ∧ (the far end reached ∨ a push into the clamp in the door's direction — a pure per-frame push memory over the glide-tolerant clamped-target read). The sustained pass gains its declared END: a quiet gap `EDGE_QUIET_MS = 350` between events resets the pass (`lastMs` in the state; per-frame `wheelEdgeSettle`) — a paused press no longer inherits its budget, and the lean has a definite moment to spring back from; the three ruled thresholds untouched. Feedback: the wheel pass LEANS the camera — a sprung first-order dolly (`src/camera/door-lean.ts`, `LEAN_MAX_M = 0.35` ∝ budget/EDGE_BUDGET_PX, rise τ 90 ms / release τ 260 ms, position and target together) that folds into the depart beat's locked door pose, so the lean continues seamlessly into the departure glide; absent under reduced motion (the enter still fires); the hidden label is unfocusable (`visibility: hidden` with the fade).
