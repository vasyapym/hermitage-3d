# Design — Military Gallery portrait atlas v2 ("the real canvases")

Design-doc register, round "atlas-v2". Supersedes atlas v1 (procedural canvases; DEC-030).
Produced by the design relay (docs/briefs/ROUNDS.md, reply to brief #2, 2026-11-01); the owner
ratified §7's decisions 1–4 and 6–10 the same day ("do everything except the merge and the full
gate run"); decision 5, the scene goldens re-baseline, happens at merge time after visual review.
Implemented on the worktree branch `atlas-v2`.

Marker legend follows the repo's register: **[sourced]** verified against a cited source;
(verify) a declared value to check at integration. Sources: the ru.wiki/en.wiki «Военная
галерея» schemes (retrieved 2026-11-01), the repo dossier of the v1 atlas law.

## 1. Design overview

- One committed KTX2 atlas, 3072×3072 px, same 21×17 grid and the same cell-index key law as
  v1; every geometric constant is v1 × 1.5 (cells 144×180). Nothing moves slots; only what is
  painted in them changes.
- Content: 322 public-domain Commons reproductions (317 Dawe half-lengths, 2 Dawe
  full-lengths for the north door pair, 3 end-wall monarchs) + 13 procedural green-silk tiles
  + neutral spare/missing cells.
- World arithmetic (from the slot law): wall frames 330 + north flanking pair 2 (ROSTER_COUNT
  332) + 3 monarch super-blocks = 335 frames. Real gallery: 332 half-lengths + 13 silk + door
  pair + end-wall triple. 330 wall frames host 317 painted + 13 silk; 15 half-lengths are
  omitted by one declared rule (§2.3 here).
- The roster is data, committed as `tools/gallery/roster.json`, keyed by Hermitage inventory
  number (`ge-NNNN`); the cell index is derived from rank by the existing slot law, never
  stored.
- The pipeline is offline: fetch → lock → compose → encode → verify. CI never fetches or
  encodes; it recomputes the composition law in mock mode, checks the committed KTX2 against
  the lockfile's sha256 and the caps, and checks roster/lock/attribution consistency.
- Sources are never committed; cached under `.cache/gallery-sources/` (gitignored).
  Committed: roster.json, hang.source.json, atlas.lock.json, atlas.digest.json, the KTX2, the
  generated ATTRIBUTIONS block, the regenerated kit GLB (uv0).
- The determinism chain is dependency-light: node `zlib` inflate for PNG sources, pinned
  `jpeg-js` for JPEG decode, our own integer box-filter resampler and tone LUT (pure,
  regex-fenced), toktx 4.4.2 with `--threads 1` and its own `--genmipmap` resampler from ONE
  composed base PNG (the proven encode path; our per-level PNG chain stays as
  preview/verification output only).
- Tone policy: per-source luminance normalisation only (black point + clamped gain in linear
  light); no colour correction; silk and monarchs under the same law.
- The runtime change is one line: `atlas.anisotropy = min(8, maxAnisotropy)` in
  rooms/1812/room.ts. Loader, colour space, material, draw count, lightmaps and uv1 are
  untouched.
- Kit GLB uv0 is regenerated by the existing gltf tool from the v2 constants (inset fraction
  5/96 → 7/144); uv1 and bakes untouched.
- Caps: predicted atlas 1.26–1.73 MB. textureBytesMax 1,200,000 → 2,650,000; test fence
  ATLAS_BYTES_MAX 700,000 → 1,900,000; payloadBytesMax 4,000,000 unchanged (predicted total
  3.3–3.6 MB). Over-prediction fallback: the declared qlevel ladder 128 → 112 → 96.
- Legal posture A (Commons PD-Art files), gated at lock time by an allowlist of Commons
  license templates; non-commercial status is an owner confirmation.
- Goldens: scene baselines M0 ×3 re-baselined by explicit decision after merge; DOM fixtures
  unaffected.
- Mock-first gate stands: `paintAtlas({ tiles: mockTiles })` is what CI pins; the real
  composition sha is pinned too and verified offline by `gallery:verify`.
- Charter: painted 2D likenesses are not "figures"; reproductions of paintings are not
  photographic textures of the interior; both distinctions are written into the register row
  (atlas-v2-charter-distinction).

## 2. The roster

2.1 Committed form: `tools/gallery/roster.json` (UTF-8, LF, 2-space, keys sorted by the
generator; a test asserts the file equals its own re-serialisation). Header: `law`
("military-gallery-1812/roster/v2"), `orientation` (realWallA west, realWallB east, walk
north->south, row0 lowest), `omitRule` ("row-tail-painted"), `frames` (3 monarchs with
symbolic slots portal/westAlly/eastAlly + 332 small frames with wall/row/col), `omitted` (15
entries with their real positions).

2.2 Key law. `key` = `ge-<digits>` (ascii form of «ГЭ-…»); silk keys `silk-<latin surname>`.
Keys are unique across the file. The atlas remains KEYED BY CELL INDEX: the composer resolves
`rank → cell` through the slot law (6…343 row-major minus the super-block footprints 21–26;
flanking pair 342/343; super-blocks 0/2/4). No cell index is ever written to roster or lock; a
test asserts the rank→cell map is a bijection onto the v1 slot set.

2.3 Filling order — anchors + one rule. Anchors: portal = Krüger, Alexander I (equestrian);
westAlly = Krafft, Franz I; eastAlly = Krüger, Friedrich Wilhelm III; north flanking pair 342
= Kutuzov, 343 = Barclay de Tolly (both Dawe full-lengths, cover-cropped; see §3.3). The rule
("row-tail-painted"): transcribe the ru.wiki hanging scheme into a table (wall, row, col from
the Kutuzov–Barclay door end, silk flag). For each (wall,row) take the world count w = 33 and
the real list R (length r); keep frames in order from the north; when r > w, drop the
SOUTHMOST PAINTED frames until w remain — silk frames are never dropped. If r < w for a row,
the shortfall is filled with the dropped frames of the adjacent row, southmost first. The
dropped-and-unconsumed frames are listed in `omitted` with their real position so the
omission is auditable. Orientation mapping real→world is a header field (one edit flips it);
default: the real wall that carries the Kutuzov–Barclay door is the world north end, rows
counted from the lowest.

TRANSCRIPTION CORRECTION (recorded 2026-11-01, the committed hang.source.json): the ru.wiki
schemes carry 342 wall slots (329 painted + 13 silk), not the 345 (332 painted + 13 silk) the
draft assumed — "записать 345 кадров из этого источника невозможно без выдумывания трёх
живописных портретов" (the transcription's own note). The arithmetic closes at 342: per wall
the real rows run 35·4 + 31, so rows 0–3 shed 2 painted each (8 drops/wall), the top row's
shortfall of 2 takes row 3's drops (the drops travel UP one row — "the next row up" names the
drops' travel direction), and 6 frames per wall stay omitted: omitted.length = 12, painted
small = 329 − 6 + 2 door pair = 319 (unchanged), silk = 13 (unchanged), roster total 335
(unchanged). The test pin moved 15 → 12 with the transcription's note as the provenance.

2.4 End-pair ruling. The two ru.wiki schemes disagree (Konstantin/Kutuzov vs
Wellington/Barclay). Ruled: Kutuzov (342) and Barclay de Tolly (343) — the two
commanders-in-chief, both Dawe, the pair every guide text describes at the gallery's door.
Rationale is recorded in the register row; either alternative is a two-key edit of roster.json
plus two lock entries. Owner decision #3.

2.5 The 13 silk: Юрковский, Каратаев, Алексополь, Балла, Н. В. Давыдов, Всеволожский, Гурьев,
Наний, Потапов, Иванов, Жевахов, Е. В. Давыдов, Лавров — at the positions the scheme gives
them, surviving the rule unconditionally. A test asserts `frames.filter(kind==='silk').length
=== 13` and that the 13 keys equal this list.

## 3. The atlas law v2 (`tools/gallery/atlas-law.mjs`, pure)

3.1 Constants (all v1 × 1.5 where geometric): ATLAS_SIZE_PX 3072; ATLAS_COLUMNS 21;
ATLAS_ROWS 17 (3024×3060 used); SLOT_W_PX 144; SLOT_H_PX 180; ATLAS_GUTTER_PX 3;
ATLAS_INSET_PX 4 (UV inset 7 px, v-flipped); content rect per cell 130×166; SUPER_BLOCK_SLOTS
2 (2×2 cells = 288×360, content 274×346); SUPER_BLOCK_COUNT 3; MISSING_SLOT 356; SLOT_COUNT
357; ROSTER_COUNT 332; MONARCH_X_SCALE 0.8/0.814 unchanged; SALT_1812
'hermitage-3d/military-gallery-1812/portrait-atlas/v2'; MIP_LEVELS 12 (3072→1); new:
SILK_COUNT 13, ATLAS_LAW_VERSION 2, QLEVEL_LADDER [128,112,96].

Why 3072 and not 4096: 4096² ETC1S of photographic content is predicted at 2.2–3.1 MB (v1
measured 0.6 bpp on flat procedural content; paintings run ≈ 0.8–1.1 bpp), which breaches
payloadBytesMax 4,000,000 as well; 3072² gives 1.5× linear resolution (130×166 visible texels
vs 86×110) at a predicted 1.26–1.73 MB and keeps the payload cap. At the west-wall distance
(4.21 m, 1080p) a canvas is ≈187 px tall → 0.89 texel/px (v1: 0.59). Monarchs: 274×346 texels
at 3.56 m (≈680 px tall) — soft but Franz I's 880-px source is the real limit.

Why uniform, not visibility-weighted: the 2.02 m east minimum exists only on the M3→M4
diagonal; along M0–M3 the east wall is the FARTHER wall. A per-wall weighting would privilege
the wrong wall for most of the walk and would replace the fenced uniform law with a two-region
law for no net gain.

3.2 Per-cell content law. Source → decode (sRGB 8-bit) → cover-crop, centred, to the content
rect aspect, so Dawe scans (aspect 0.86–0.89) lose ≈7–10% width symmetrically (the real frame
rebate hides about that much) → integer box-filter downsample in linear light to the content
rect → tone law → write; gutter (3 px) and inset (4 px) band painted by the band law (inset
band edge-replicates the content, gutter stays flat — v1's gutter-paint law, widened). Cells
344–355 (spare) and 356 (missing): v1 neutral paint, unchanged.

3.3 Monarch texture law. Alexander I → portal super-block; Franz I → westAlly; Friedrich
Wilhelm III → eastAlly. Same content law at super-block size. Sources: Commons 1361×1920 /
880×1255 / 1335×1920 — all ≥ 2× the 274×346 target except Franz I (≈3.2×, fine). North pair:
Dawe full-lengths (real aspect ≈0.74) cover-cropped to the content rect lose ≈7% height
top/bottom.

3.4 Silk tile law (procedural, in the pure module, integer ops only). Palette (adds 3 tokens
to the atlas palette): SILK_A #2e4a36, SILK_B #3b5c44, SILK_EDGE #1f3326. Weave: `((x + y) &
1) ? A : B` — the 50/50 blend of the two tones — with a horizontal sheen ramp (brightness +0
at the cell's vertical centre line to −10/255 at the edges, linear in |x − cx|); hash-noise
±3/255 from the existing hash law seeded with SALT_1812 + key; a 2-px SILK_EDGE border inside
the content rect. Identical for all 13 tiles except the noise seed.

3.5 Tone policy. Per source, in linear light via integer LUTs: black point = 1st percentile
luminance mapped to L 0.01; gain so that the 98th percentile lands at L 0.80, clamped to
[0.70, 1.40]; no chroma/white-balance change; no sharpening. Chosen parameters are written
into the lock per key (`tone: { black, gain }`) so a re-compose does not depend on
recomputing statistics. Monarchs, door pair: same law. Silk: untouched. Goal: the two Commons
scan families (≈1288×1476 and ≈1650×1920) sit side by side without exposure steps; varnish
colour differences are accepted as the paintings' own.

3.6 Mip/filter/anisotropy. The encoded chain is toktx's own `--genmipmap` resampler from the
composed base PNG at the pinned version (deterministic at 4.4.2 with `--threads 1`); our own
box-filter chain is written per level for preview/verification. Runtime: trilinear as today;
`anisotropy = Math.min(8, renderer.capabilities.getMaxAnisotropy())` on the atlas texture
only (grazing angles down the gallery dominate; zero bytes).

3.7 Cap arithmetic (chosen numbers). Atlas predicted: 3072² with mips = 12.6 Mpx; at
0.8–1.1 bpp → 1.26–1.73 MB. Ceiling fence ATLAS_BYTES_MAX = 1,900,000. Textures: other ktx2 =
1,131,373 − 418,982 = 712,391 B; + ≤ 1,900,000 → ≤ 2,612,391. textureBytesMax = 2,650,000.
Payload: 1.86–2.0 MB today − 0.42 + ≤ 1.9 → ≤ 3.5 MB; payloadBytesMax 4,000,000 unchanged
(ROOM_CAP_MAX 8,000,000 untouched). GPU memory: 3072² transcoded (ETC1/BC1/ASTC 4×4) ≈ 4.5 MB
+ mips 6 MB; NPOT with mips is WebGL2-legal and all KTX2Loader targets block-pad. Ladder: if
the measured KTX2 exceeds 1,900,000 B at qlevel 128, `gallery:encode` steps down the
QLEVEL_LADDER and records the level used in the lock; if 96 still exceeds, stop and report
(owner decision, no silent loosening).

3.8 Mock-first gate. Stands. `paintAtlas({ tiles })` takes a tile provider; `mockTiles` (pure:
v1-style procedural canvases via the hash law) is the default CI mode; the real composer
supplies image tiles from the lock + cache. Silk, gutters, spare/missing paint are identical
in both modes, so the mock run exercises the whole law except the source pixels.

## 4. The pipeline (`tools/gallery/`, node ≥ 20, offline)

4.1 Stages and scripts (package.json, dispatcher `tools/gallery/import.mjs`):
- `gallery:transcribe` — reads the checked-in `hang.source.json` (hand-transcribed from the
  ru.wiki scheme: 345 half-length frames with wall/row/col/silk/ГЭ/Commons title, plus the 5
  anchors) and writes roster.json by the §2.3 rule. Pure function `applyHang(hang,
  worldCounts)` in `hang-law.mjs` (pure, tested).
- `gallery:fetch` — for every painted key: Commons API
  (`prop=imageinfo&iiprop=url|sha1|size|mime|extmetadata`) → original URL, sha1, width/height,
  `LicenseShortName`/template; downloads to `.cache/gallery-sources/<sha256>.<ext>`; rejects
  files whose template is not in LICENSE_ALLOWLIST = ['PD-Art', 'PD-old-100',
  'PD-old-auto-expired', 'PD-old', 'CC-PD-Mark'] (a "missing SDC status" flag alone does not
  reject; a missing template does).
- `gallery:lock` — writes `atlas.lock.json` from the cache: per key `{ commonsTitle, url,
  commonsSha1, sha256, bytes, width, height, mime, license, tone: { black, gain }, crop: [x,
  y, w, h] }`; `toolchain: { node, 'jpeg-js': <exact>, toktx: '4.4.2', flags: '…' }`;
  `artifact: { sha256, bytes, width: 3072, height: 3072, levels: 12, qlevel }` (filled by
  encode).
- `gallery:compose [--mock]` — lock + cache → RGBA 3072² + the level PNG chain
  `out/military-gallery/level-N.png` (the hand-rolled stored-deflate PNG encoder) → updates
  `atlas.digest.json` (mock: the §4.3 mock section; real: the composed section).
- `gallery:encode` — the proven path: `toktx --t2 --encode etc1s --clevel 1 --qlevel <ladder>
  --threads 1 --genmipmap --assign_oetf srgb --assign_primaries bt709 <ktx2>
  out/military-gallery/level-0.png` (ONE base PNG; toktx's own resampler builds the chain);
  writes the artifact sha/bytes into the digest and the lock; steps the ladder on the §3.7
  fence.
- `gallery:verify` — re-reads the cache by lock (sha256 must match, else "source changed
  upstream, re-lock"), re-composes, re-encodes to a temp file, byte-compares with the
  committed KTX2; exits non-zero on any diff.
- `gallery:attrib` — regenerates the ATTRIBUTIONS block between
  `<!-- gallery-atlas:begin/end -->`.
- `gallery:report` — prints atlas bytes vs ATLAS_BYTES_MAX, texture/payload totals vs the
  manifest.
- `gallery:all` = transcribe → fetch → lock → compose → encode → attrib → report.

4.2 Committed vs cached. Committed: roster.json, hang.source.json, atlas.lock.json,
atlas.digest.json, the KTX2, the ATTRIBUTIONS block, the regenerated kit GLB (uv0).
Cached/gitignored: `.cache/gallery-sources/`, `out/`. Never committed: any source image, any
PNG level.

4.3 `atlas.digest.json` v2: `{ law: 2, mock: { rgbaSha256, pngSha256, pngBytes }, composed: {
rgbaSha256 }, artifact: { sha256, bytes } }` — sections nullable while pending. `mock.*` is
CI-recomputed; `composed`/`artifact` are offline-verified and cross-checked against the lock
in CI.

4.4 Determinism fences: no sharp/libvips; JPEG via pinned jpeg-js (exact version in
devDependencies and in the lock; verify refuses a mismatch); PNG via node zlib inflate
(lossless, implementation-independent); resampler and tone LUT are integer-only in
`resample.mjs` (pure, regex-fenced); toktx version string checked (`toktx --version`) before
encode; `--threads 1`.

## 8. Risks + challenges

- JPEG decode drift: different decoders differ by ±1 LSB; mitigated by pinning jpeg-js
  exactly and recording it in the lock; verify refuses a version mismatch. Failure case:
  jpeg-js unmaintained → swap decoder = re-lock + re-pin (declared, not silent).
- Commons overwrites: a re-uploaded file breaks sha256 at fetch; the lock pins bytes, not
  titles, so the failure is loud and local to one key. Tiny-scan sitter (395×453): accepted
  (upsampling ≈ 3× to 130×166 content is near-native); if the owner objects, that key is a
  candidate for a row-tail swap recorded in `omitted`.
- NPOT 3072 through toktx/KTX2Loader: block formats pad; the encode path is toktx's own
  `--genmipmap` chain; if toktx rejects NPOT levels, the fallback is decision #7's 4096²
  variant.
- Bytes above prediction: ladder 128 → 112 → 96, recorded; if 96 still exceeds 1,900,000 B,
  stop and report — no cap edit without a new ruling.
- Tone law too timid/aggressive across scan families: parameters are per-key in the lock and
  clamped; a hand override (`toneOverride`) is permitted and recorded.
- Mip bleed: at level ≥ 3 cells are ≤ 18 px wide and gutters < 1 px; the band's edge
  replication at compose time limits it; frames at that mip are > 15 m away. Same character
  as v1, scaled.
- Positional fidelity is approximate by construction (330 vs 345 wall frames); the register
  says so, and `omitted` makes the loss auditable rather than hidden.
- Scheme ambiguity (door pair): ruled, reversible (two keys).
- Russian museum law: non-commercial posture; low-but-not-zero residual; the lockfile makes
  removal of any file a one-step re-lock.
- CI runtime: mock compose at 3072² + 11 levels + sha256 ≈ 8 s in node; acceptable. The KTX2
  is read once for header/sha.
- RU lazy chunk budget: +≈230 B; measured by the existing budget check.
- Rejected: 4096² uniform (bytes blow payloadBytesMax; qlevel ≤ 80 to fit looks worse than
  3072@128); visibility-weighted two-region atlas (wrong wall privileged along M0–M3, fenced
  law complexity); two atlases per wall (no cell-count gain at POT sizes); UASTC (8 bpp, ≈ 4×
  bytes); sharp/libvips resampling (platform float drift); runtime fetch of sources
  (committed-assets rule); posture B (supply incomplete, 1981 monograph scans are not cleaner
  than Commons PD-Art); storing cell indices in the roster (second source of truth against
  the slot law).

## INTEGRATION CORRECTIONS (the real-fetch runs, 2026-11-01, orchestrator)

1. The imageinfo `sha1` integrity check compared a BASE64 digest against the API's field —
   Commons' `sha1` is hexadecimal (40 chars); the check failed on every file. Fixed to the hex
   digest (verified on ge-7804: the downloaded bytes hash exactly to the API value).
2. The license gate's literal template list rejected real family members — e.g. ge-7806
   (Friedrich Wilhelm III, Krüger) carries `{{PD-Art-old-100-expired}}`, and the roster's
   sample showed `{{PD-old-70}}`. The allowlist became the FAMILY-STEM law
   `LICENSE_STEMS = ['pd-art', 'pd-old', 'cc-pd-mark']` (a template matches when its name
   equals a stem or continues it with '-'); the lock records the full template name found;
   the lock↔roster test asserts `isFamilyLicense`. The §6.3 posture is unchanged.
